Import from Garmin

Garmin has no personal API, so this reads the CSV you export from Garmin Connect. Columns are matched automatically and you can correct them before anything is added.

  1. Open Garmin Connect on the web → Activities, and scroll until every run you want is loaded.
  2. Hit Export CSV (upper right of the Activities list).
  3. Choose the file below.
